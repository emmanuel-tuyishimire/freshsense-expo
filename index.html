Actual Product

Biodegradable Smart
Chromatic Sticker

A 3×3 cm sticker that speaks freshness. Our non-toxic, plant-based smart inks react to the chemical byproducts of food degradation (volatile organic compounds, pH shifts, and microbial activity) — changing color visibly without any battery or electronics.

100% Plant-Based Inks
Anthocyanin from purple sweet potato; curcumin from local turmeric — fully GRAS-certified.
Fully Biodegradable Substrate
Cellulose-based backing decomposes in 28 days in standard compost — zero microplastic contamination.
Three-State Visual Language
Green (Fresh) → Yellow (Use Soon, within 24h) → Red (Discard). No app needed for basic readings.

New Product Development

The Local
Ecosystem Journey

From Nile Delta soil to supermarket shelf — Egypt's fully local smart freshness supply chain.

Phase 01 · Research

Ink R&D at AUC

American University in Cairo's Chemistry Dept. isolates anthocyanin pigments from Delta-grown purple sweet potato and curcumin from Fayoum turmeric — creating Egypt's first food-safe smart-ink portfolio.

Partner: AUC Chemistry & Materials Science
Timeline: Q1–Q2 2024
Output: 3 patent-pending ink formulas
Partner: Sakr Paper Industries, 10th of Ramadan City
Timeline: Q3 2024
Output: 500k substrate units/month capacity
Phase 02 · Manufacturing

Local Substrate Production

Biodegradable cellulose substrates manufactured at 10th of Ramadan City industrial zone. FSC-certified paper sourced from Nile Pulp & Paper. Ink-coating with our proprietary slot-die process.

Phase 03 · Regulatory

NFSA Certification

Full registration under National Food Safety Authority Decree 83/2019. All ink components verified food-contact safe. Halal certification from HEIA. ISO 22000 audit passed for manufacturing site.

Authority: NFSA + HEIA + ITIDA
Timeline: Q4 2024
Output: Full regulatory green-light
Partners: Seoudi Market, Spinneys Egypt, Rabbit
Timeline: Q1–Q2 2025
Scale: 12 pilot stores, Cairo & Alexandria
Phase 04 · Pilot

Hypermarket Pilot Launch

Live pilot across 12 Cairo and Alexandria hypermarket locations. Tagged product categories: deli meats, fresh dairy, seafood, and bakery. Real-time data dashboards for store managers via FreshSense Enterprise portal.

Phase 05 · Scale

National Rollout & Export

Full national rollout targeting 500+ retail points. Export pipeline to GCC markets (UAE, KSA) via Egypt's existing trade corridors. B2B licensing to regional food manufacturers.

Timeline: Q3 2025 – Q2 2026
Target: 500 retail points · 3M households
Export: UAE, KSA, Morocco
